Frequently Asked Questions!

Q: What is Chiikawa?

A: Chiikawa is a Japanese webcomic series created by Nagano. It features cute, small creatures and their daily lives.

Q: Does the word “Chiikawa” mean anything?

A: Chiikawa comes from the Japanese phrase “Nanka Chiisakute Kawaii Yatsu,” meaning “something small and cute”.

Q: Where can I find more information about Chiikawa?

A: You can visit the official Chiikawa website or follow the creator on Twitter.

Q: Are there any merchandise or products related to Chiikawa?

A: Yes, there is a lot! Chiikawa Market is the official merch store. Good luck ordering merch from Japan since the U.S. has imposed tariffs on most imports!! You can also try checking out your local Miniso!

Q: Where can I watch the Chiikawa anime?

The series finally has an official English dub as of October 2026!!!! You can watch new episodes every week on their Youtube channel.

Q: Where can I read the Chiikawa manga?

No American publishing company has licensed the manga yet!! Fans are translating the series on Twitter.
